I am a doctoral researcher at the Centre of Excellence in Music, Mind, Body and Brain at the University of Jyväskylä in Finland. I am broadly interested in studying the neuroscientific effects and mechanisms of music based interventions using (M)EEG and fMRI. During my PhD, I am examining how the somatosensory state of the body influences the music listening experience using MEG. Outside work, you can find me singing, reading or crocheting!
